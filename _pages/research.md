My research background is in computational modeling of complex systems. During my Ph.D. at Kyoto University, I developed particle-based models to study collective cell dynamics and the behavior of thousands of interacting agents.

That work shapes how I approach applied data science: make assumptions explicit, connect local behavior to system-level outcomes, and validate a model before using it to inform a decision.
