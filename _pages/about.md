- I work for **[Ocean Network Express Pte. Ltd.](https://www.one-line.com/)** as a **Data Science Manager and Digital Solution Unit Leader**, based at the **Global Headquarters in Singapore**.

- I lead the design and delivery of **applied machine learning, optimization, and decision-support systems** for real-world industrial challenges. My work includes demand forecasting, cost optimization, and large-scale analytics platforms, with a strong focus on **production readiness, scalability, and measurable business impact**.

- I have an engineering and scientific research background. Prior to industry, my work focused on **computer simulations and numerical modeling** to analyze and optimize complex systems. This training continues to inform my approach to data science, emphasizing rigorous modeling, system-level thinking, and end-to-end ownership from problem formulation to production deployment.

- I received my **Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering** from [*Kyoto University*](https://www.kyoto-u.ac.jp/ja), my **M.Sc. in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering** from [*Hong Kong University of Science and Technology*](https://www.ust.hk), and my **B.Eng. in Chemical Engineering and Technology** from [*Southwest Petroleum University*](https://www.swpu.edu.cn/en/).

- Born and raised in Sichuan, China.
